# P2.5 cocoa benchmark

Generated by `scripts/prepare_cocoa_benchmark.jl` from the immutable
Moreno-Zambrano et al. 2022 source archive. See
[the implementation notes](../../../docs/cocoa_benchmark.md).

- `datasets.csv`: all 32 trials, with the paper's 23-trial analysis subset marked.
- `<trial>.csv`: physical observations and normalized likelihood values.
- `mechanisms.csv` and `mechanism_variants.csv`: all five mechanisms and 32 combinations.
- `published_baseline.csv`: source baseline convergence summaries and BMA weights.
- `provenance.toml`: input hashes, units, and transformations.
- `fits/`: saved sequential fit attempts and comparison tables.

Missing lactic-acid observations and physical scales are empty fields.
The source's assumed normalized hidden initial zero is marked
`unobserved_initial`. Initial rows are not likelihood observations.
All measurements retain mg/g-pulp units and hours. No physical lactic-acid
predictions can be recovered for `ghhp2`–`ghhp5` without a sourced scale.

MLE training scores and the source's Bayesian PSIS-LOO are different metrics.
Unverified optimizer candidates are not accepted fits; blank published
scores are unavailable, not zero.
