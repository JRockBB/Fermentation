This repository represents the implementation of the model described in the paper "A mathematical model of cocoa bean fermentation" 
by Mauricio Moreno-Zambrano, Sergio Grimbs, Matthias S. Ullrich and Marc-Thorsten Hütt.

The codes and data here contained are presented for the sake of reproducibility of the aforementioned article and they are not intended 
to serve as a stand-alone software. Their use is at your own risk, so the authors are not responsible for any damage that may result 
from its use.
